<p class="page-note">Last updated: July 18, 2026.</p>

Not every case starts with a clean title. Some begin as a tag on the edge of another report: a bad label, a private gate, a forgotten correction, a public shell still glowing after the room has moved.

These are the folders: recurring subjects first, then the single-report traces that may become a pattern later. The difference matters. One is a case with weight; the other is a fingerprint waiting to see if it belongs to anything.
